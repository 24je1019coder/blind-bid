A game of nerve, timing, and hidden bids.

How to play

  1. Create a room and share its 4-letter code. 2–6 players can join before the host starts.
  2. Everyone starts with 100 coins. There are 8 prize cards, shuffled each game.
  3. Each round, secretly bid any whole number from 0 up to your remaining coins. You have 20 seconds. No bid means 0.
  4. All bids are revealed together. The unique highest positive bid wins the prize points and pays their bid.
  5. If the highest bids tie, nobody wins and those prize points carry to the next round. A zero bid never wins.
  6. After 8 rounds, most points wins. Ties in points are broken by the most coins left.
  7. Refresh to reconnect using this browser's saved session. The server is the authority on bids, coins, and scores.
